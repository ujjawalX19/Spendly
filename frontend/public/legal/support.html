Vittova support

For the Vittova Android app (com.vittova.app) and web app.

Contact us

Email support@vittova.in from the address on your account. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Please don't send passwords, full card numbers or bank details: we never need them.

Common answers

  • I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email. Check spam, wait a few minutes and try again. Links work once and expire.
  • Google sign-in opened a browser. That can happen on some phones; finish signing in and tap Open Vittova to return to the app.
  • Payments aren't detected automatically. Detection is optional, Android only, and works with supported UPI and bank apps after you allow notification access. You can always add expenses yourself.
  • Where do my numbers come from? From the budget, bills and expenses you add. Vittova doesn't connect to your bank and doesn't track income or bank balances.
  • Vittova Pro. Pro is not available to buy yet. When it is, it is sold through Google Play and managed or cancelled in Google Play.

Your data

  • Export all your expenses as a CSV file from Profile in the app.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account if you can't sign in.
  • What we collect and why is in the Privacy Policy.
