Vittova is for people aged 18 and over. It's a budgeting and money-awareness app, not a bank, wallet or lender.

What you can do with Vittova

🧾

Track daily expenses

Add a spend in a few taps, snap a receipt, or let Vittova add payments automatically from supported UPI apps on Android.

📊

Understand your spending

See which categories take the most, and how this month compares with the last.

🎯

Stay within a budget

Set a monthly budget. Safe-to-Spend shows what's left per day after bills and savings.

⏳

See how long money lasts

Burn Rate warns you early when you're on pace to run out before the month ends.

🔥

Build good habits

A daily mission, a Money Streak and short challenges like a week without food delivery.

💬

Learn the basics

Ask Vittova plain questions about budgeting and saving. Answers start from your own numbers.

Hostel, flatmates and trips

Group Pool splits shared bills with an invite code, shows who owes whom and records settle-ups, so shared money doesn't turn into awkward conversations.

How it works

  1. Track. Add expenses, scan receipts or import a bank statement PDF.
  2. Understand. Vittova puts your budget, bills and spending pace into one picture.
  3. Decide. Know what you can spend today and whether a purchase fits your month.

Free to start

Tracking, budgets, Safe-to-Spend, Money Streak and challenges are free. Vittova Pro adds unlimited decision checks and deeper tools, and isn't on sale yet.

Vittova gives general financial education, not investment advice, and doesn't replace advice from a parent, guardian or a qualified professional. Read how we handle your data in the Privacy Policy.
